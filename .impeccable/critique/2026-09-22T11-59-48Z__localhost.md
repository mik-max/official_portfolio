## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment (Assessment A):** Specific at the top of the page, generic in the middle, specific again at the Interlude, generic again at Contact. The git-log Experience section, the build.log/notes-to-self.md devices, and the numbered markers are real, product-specific choices. But Skills abandons that invented language completely — it's a plain grouped-pill grid, the single most generic "tech stack" pattern in developer portfolios, and could be dropped into any site unchanged.

**Deterministic scan (Assessment B):** The static/regex CLI scan (`impeccable detect --json src/components src/app`) came back clean (exit 0, zero findings) — it can't see runtime layout, so it's not evidence of quality, just of nothing pattern-matchable in raw JSX. **Where they agree:** both independently flag Skills — A on grounds of genericness, B on grounds of literal 10px text in its tag chips. Two different methods landing on the same section is a strong signal, not a coincidence.

**Where the detector caught something the LLM missed:** the `undersized-ui-text` and `nested-cards` counts are new, mechanical ground A didn't cite by name.

**False positives, my read as synthesizer:**
- `overused-font` — confirmed intentional; git log shows `42d891e redesign(type): switch to single-family Space Grotesk, confirmed`. Not a bug.
- `image-hover-transform` — a deliberate, fairly tasteful micro-interaction (card/photo hover scale), not an actual anti-pattern. I'd leave this alone.
- `text-occlusion` — I looked closely at what text was supposedly hidden: "✦ nested cards" and "undersized functional te[xt]". Those aren't real site copy — they're the **other two rules' own diagnostic labels**. This strongly reads as the detector's debug overlays occluding each other as the page scrolls, not actual invisible content on the site. I'm not treating this as a real bug; flagging it here so it's not silently dropped either.
- `dark-glow` (#ffba00) — Assessment B couldn't find this color anywhere in the source (not in globals.css, not as a Tailwind class, not a CSS var), and it doesn't match our accent-only blue palette at all. Unresolved — possibly a detector artifact, possibly a browser-default link/visited color bleeding through. Worth a manual look, not worth acting on blind.

## Overall Impression

The site has real ideas — the git-log Experience section and the Dark Interlude are the strongest evidence this wasn't templated. But craft execution is uneven: the accent-color-as-opacity approach to secondary text (`ink/40`, `/50`, `/60`) fails contrast in multiple places, Skills never actually got the redesign it was supposed to get, and there's a factual inconsistency (Collabify tagged "LIVE" next to a screenshot that says "Coming soon") that undercuts the craft/attention-to-detail claim the site itself is making about its author.

## What's Working

1. **Experience-as-git-log** — commit-style dates, company as byline, one pulled metric per role. It earns its own "Proof, not adjectives" heading instead of just stating it.
2. **The Dark Interlude** — the one section that couldn't be mistaken for another portfolio's; specific, restrained copy, well-placed as a single tonal break.
3. **Numbered markers over eyebrows** — genuinely executed at the section-header level, which makes it more conspicuous when Contact/Footer quietly revert to tracked-uppercase labels for sub-fields.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] No mobile navigation.** `Navbar.tsx:34` wraps the entire link list in `hidden lg:flex` with no drawer/hamburger anywhere in the codebase. Below 1024px, About/Skills/Experience/Projects are unreachable except by scrolling past them or hitting the footer at the very bottom.
**Why it matters:** a recruiter opening this on a phone loses all wayfinding for ~90% of the page.
**Fix:** add a mobile menu triggered from the existing nav bar, reusing `navItems`.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

**[P1] Hero states its own numbers twice in one viewport.** The `build.log` terminal panel lists the same 3 of 4 stats that the row directly below the CTAs repeats seconds later — both visible without scrolling on desktop.
**Why it matters:** undercuts whether the hero-metric pattern is earned; reads as padding, not confidence.
**Fix:** pick one device — let the terminal carry the proof, or the stat row, not both.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

**[P1] Skills never got the redesign.** It's still a plain grouped-pill grid — no window-chrome, no dark treatment — the exact pattern flagged as "too flat and basic" that prompted a redesign concept (a `stack.json` code window) that was mocked but never shipped to code. Independently corroborated by the detector's `undersized-ui-text` hits landing disproportionately on this section's tag chips.
**Why it matters:** this is the section most likely to make a reviewer think "templated," directly undermining design-specificity.
**Fix:** ship the `stack.json` code-window treatment already mocked on the design canvas.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset` + `$impeccable layout`

**[P1] Secondary text fails WCAG AA contrast in multiple places.** Computed against paper `#F4F1E9`: `ink/60` ≈ 4.43:1 (Hero description, Contact subhead — both fail the 4.5:1 normal-text floor by a hair), `ink/50` ≈ 3.27:1 (badges, stat labels, Skills category labels — well under 4.5:1), `ink/40` ≈ 2.47:1 (section numerals, footer text, project URLs — fails outright).
**Why it matters:** this is real accessibility non-compliance, not a taste call, and it's the exact "Sam" persona's dependency.
**Fix:** stop deriving secondary text from ink-opacity alone; use a fixed darker value, or raise the opacity floor to ~`/70` for anything under 16px.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

**[P2] "LIVE" badge on Collabify contradicts its own screenshot.** `Projects.tsx` hardcodes `LIVE` on every card, including Collabify, whose thumbnail literally reads "Coming soon."
**Why it matters:** a visible factual inconsistency on a portfolio about a frontend engineer's craft/attention to detail is a bad look, and it's the kind of thing a stress-testing viewer catches in five seconds.
**Fix:** make the badge data-driven per project instead of hardcoded, and set Collabify's status honestly.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

**[P2] `target="_blank"` without `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.** Contact.tsx:22 and Projects.tsx:21. Trivial, low-severity, worth catching.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`
